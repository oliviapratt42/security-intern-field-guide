Career branch

Threat Intelligence

Threat intelligence turns threat information into context, confidence, and recommendations that help the right audience make better decisions.

Good fit if you like research and judgment CTF fit: reverse, traffic, web Core habit: separate evidence from guesses

What this branch does

Threat intelligence helps organizations understand which threats matter to them. Analysts may track actors, campaigns, malware, vulnerabilities, tactics, infrastructure, and public reporting, then turn that information into something useful for defenders, leaders, or partner teams.

A list of indicators is not automatically intelligence. The value comes from relevance, confidence, context, and a clear decision or action.

Common work

  • Track threat actors, campaigns, vulnerabilities, malware families, and tactics.
  • Read reporting and summarize what is relevant to the organization.
  • Enrich indicators with context, source quality, and confidence levels.
  • Help detection teams map behavior to MITRE ATT&CK.
  • Produce briefings for technical teams, managers, or executives.

CTF skills that transfer

Reverse engineering helps with malware behavior and file analysis. Traffic analysis helps with infrastructure and command-and-control patterns. Web and access-control challenges help analysts understand how vulnerabilities are exploited in campaigns.

Standards and reference frameworks

  • MITRE ATT&CK for behavior-based threat language.
  • STIX and TAXII for structured cyber threat intelligence sharing.
  • CISA Known Exploited Vulnerabilities Catalog (KEV) for exploited vulnerability context.
